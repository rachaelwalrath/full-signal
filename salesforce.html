Why the AI is not doing much yet

Why the setup comes first

The AI in Salesforce works from what is in Salesforce. In a new org that is nothing yet: no pipeline, no customer history, no cases. Until your leads, deals and customer conversations live there, the AI has nothing to work with. So the setup comes first.

Questions

Questions about Salesforce implementation for small business

We just bought Salesforce. Where do we start?

With a discovery session on how your team sells or serves customers today. The setup is built around that.

Can you bring in our data from spreadsheets?

Yes. A basic data import is included. What it covers is agreed in writing before we start.

Which Salesforce edition do we need?

The one that fits what you need built. I check your edition before I scope anything, because it decides what can be built. Licenses are bought from Salesforce and are not part of the price.

Salesforce has its own setup agent now. Why would we need you?

It builds what you ask it for. Someone still has to know what to ask: which objects to use, what your edition allows, what the next phase will need. That is the work.

What if we are slow getting you data?

The four weeks pause for delays on your side. Those are named in the agreement before we start.

Is Iris included?

No. Iris is a separate monthly subscription and is not part of a starter package. Contact us if you want her.

We already use Salesforce and it needs fixing. Is this for us?

Not as a starter package. That work is scoped after a look at your setup. Book a call and I'll tell you what it would take.

We are on HubSpot and not sure about Salesforce. Where do we start?

With the CRM Scalability Assessment: a 45-minute meeting, then a written evaluation of staying on HubSpot against moving to Salesforce within 48 hours. It costs $799, credited toward the work on whichever path you choose.
