Why the AI sits unused

When leads still live in a spreadsheet, stages mean different things to different people, and customer emails never become cases, there is little for the AI to summarize or draft from.

Handed over at launch

Meet Iris, your Salesforce admin

Iris is a Claude skill built from your own Salesforce setup. She knows your objects, pipeline stages, queues, flows and reports, because she was written from them.

What she does

Ask her how something works or how to change it, and she walks your team through it step by step:

  • Add a new user
  • Change a pipeline stage
  • Fix a report that shows the wrong numbers
  • Explain what a flow does and when it runs

What she does not do

She does not log in to Salesforce or make changes herself. Your team makes each change, with Iris explaining the steps. She holds no passwords or login details.

Iris is AI. Using her needs a Claude plan (Pro or Team). If your team is new to Claude, we can set that up as a short add-on.
