Example

We want a Negotiation stage between Proposal and Closed Won. How do I add it?

Here is how to do it in your setup:
  1. In Setup, open Object Manager, then Opportunity, then Fields & Relationships, and click Stage.
  2. Click New, name the stage Negotiation, and give it a probability between the ones on Proposal and Closed Won.
  3. Open Sales Processes, edit your Standard Sales process, and move Negotiation into the selected values.
  4. Back on the Stage field, click Reorder and place it after Proposal.
Your pipeline dashboard groups deals by stage, so Negotiation shows up there once a deal moves into it.
