FAQ

Before you book

What happens in the AI Assessment?

A 45-minute interview with you about how your business runs. Within 48 hours you get a visual AI Assessment report: your quick wins, 3–7 recommended tools with cost, setup time, and hours saved, a 4-day plan, and the financial impact. Then we walk through it together on a review call. If we don't find at least 5 hours a week of time savings, you get a full refund. It costs $999, and that fee counts toward any package or training engagement you sign, with no expiry.

How much time will our team need to give?

The AI Assessment needs 45 minutes from you. The Audit needs a decision-maker plus 3–5 hours of interviews. The Sprint needs one internal owner for 2–3 hours a week. The Brain needs your expert for about 20 sessions over three months, plus an executive sponsor.

What happens to our data and knowledge?

It stays yours. We set access rules and guardrails as part of every engagement, and the knowledge system we build belongs to your firm.

What's the difference between the Claude training track and the packages?

The packages (Audit, Sprint, Brain) are built for you. The Claude training track teaches you and your team to set up and run Claude yourselves. You can do both. A Team Claude Rollout is a good way to find out which workflows are worth a Signal Sprint.
