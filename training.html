FAQ

Questions about the training

What's the difference between the Claude training track and the packages?

The packages (Audit, Sprint, Brain) are built for you. The Claude training track teaches you and your team to set up and run Claude yourselves. You can do both. A Team Claude Rollout is a good way to find out which workflows are worth a Signal Sprint.

Do I need a paid Claude plan for the training?

For the Lab and the 1:1, a paid individual plan is recommended so you can use Projects, Skills, and connectors fully. For a Team Claude Rollout, your company will need a Claude Team or Enterprise plan. We'll help you choose the right one.

What if I can't make the webinar live?

Register anyway. Everyone who signs up gets the replay and the free Claude Setup Checklist.

What happens if the Lab fills up?

Cohort 1 is capped at 20 people so everyone gets hands-on help. Once it fills, you can join the waitlist and get first access to the next cohort.

Will sessions be recorded?

Yes. Every Lab session is recorded, and you keep the templates and 30 days of group support after the final session.

Can I just ask a few questions first?

Yes. Book a free Q&A call to bring your questions about AI or about working with Full Signal. There is no cost and no commitment.
