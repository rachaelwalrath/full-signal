What you leave with

Your business, written into Claude once, so it stops starting from zero.

Out of the box, Claude knows nothing about your clients, your voice or the way you do things, so every answer comes back generic. Over four weeks you fix that on your own work, one session at a time.

  • A Project that knows your business

    Your services, your clients, your writing samples and your rules, in one place Claude reads every time.

  • Three skills for the tasks you keep re-typing

    The proposal, the client update, the follow-up. Written down once, done your way every time after.

  • Your inbox and calendar connected

    Claude can see what is on your plate today, so it works from your week instead of a blank page.

  • A daily brief you use the next morning

    Type two words and see your day and week in one place, without clicking through app after app.

The four sessions

One build a week, on your own tasks.

  1. Session 1

    Foundations

    Which Claude plan and model fit how you work, your profile, and one task from your week done live. Homework: gather your writing samples and company documents.

  2. Session 2

    Build your Project

    The description, the instructions, your voice samples, your company information and memory, so Claude answers like someone who works with you.

  3. Session 3

    From prompts to skills

    Take the tasks you keep re-typing and turn them into skills you can run in a line.

  4. Session 4

    Connections and rhythm

    Connect your inbox and calendar, build your daily brief skill, and use it the next morning.

After every session you get the recording, a step-by-step written guide and the templates, so missing a live session doesn't leave you behind. Questions between sessions go in a shared Slack group, open until 30 days after the last session.

How you'll know it worked

Count the hours before and after.

In session one you name three tasks that eat your week and roughly how long each takes. In session four you time the same three again. You leave with your own hours-back number, worked out on your own week.

Who it's for

Owners and operators who sell their own expertise.

If clients pay for what you know, and the same handful of tasks eats your week, this is built around you. You don't need to be technical. You need a paid Claude plan and a few tasks you'd like off your plate.

Not for anyone looking for coding or automation-platform training, or for people who sell AI services.

Questions

Which Claude plan do I need?

A paid Claude plan, in place before the first session. Session one covers which plan and model fit how you work, so if you're unsure, bring the question.

How much time does it take each week?

The live session is 90 minutes. Between sessions there is a short piece of homework that builds on your own work, such as gathering writing samples or picking the tasks to turn into skills.

What if I miss a session?

You get the recording, the written guide and the templates within a day of every session, and you can ask questions in the Slack group.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Finish week four with your Project, three working skills and a running daily brief, or join the next cohort free.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Everything is set up in plain English, with no code.

What happens after the four weeks?

You keep everything you built. Graduates can join the Signal Tune-Up, a monthly group session to keep your setup current as Claude changes.
