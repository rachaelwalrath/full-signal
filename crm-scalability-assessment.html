CRM Scalability Assessment

HubSpot or Salesforce? Know in 48 hours, in writing.

Your team has started asking whether HubSpot will hold up as you grow. Sit down with me for 45 minutes. Within 48 hours you have a written evaluation of staying against moving: what each one fixes, what each one costs, and which fits where your company is headed. Sometimes the answer is to stay.

Book a CRM Scalability Assessment

45-minute meeting · Written evaluation within 48 hours · Book first, pay after the session

The conversation that keeps coming back

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • You need to track something that is not a contact, a company or a deal, and find that custom objects need an Enterprise subscription.
  • Before every pipeline review, someone exports deals to a spreadsheet to build the forecast.
  • Each new hire restarts the conversation about what the next plan up would cost.
  • Someone says "we should just move to Salesforce," and nobody in the room can say what that would cost or how long it would take.

Source for the first line: HubSpot Knowledge Base, How to use objects for business processes. Checked 2026-11-13.

What you get

HubSpot or Salesforce, laid out side by side

  1. A 45-minute meeting on what your business needs, your growth plan and where the current system gets in your way.
  2. A written evaluation within 48 hours of the meeting.
  3. The case for staying on HubSpot and the case for moving to Salesforce, with the pros and cons of each.
  4. The fixes worth making in HubSpot if you stay, and what they would cost.
  5. The Salesforce license that fits if you move.

Why the answer is fair

No license to sell on either side

  • I don't sell HubSpot or Salesforce licenses. Neither answer earns me a commission.
  • Before founding Full Signal, I was a Salesforce Solutions Architect. I have integrated HubSpot with Salesforce, and I have migrated a company from HubSpot to Salesforce.
  • The answer may be to stay. When it is, the evaluation says so in plain words.

The price

$799. Book first, pay after the session.

If you hire Full Signal for either path, the $799 is credited toward that work: a Salesforce starter package if you move, or the fixes in HubSpot if you stay.

The written evaluation arrives within 48 hours of the meeting.

For and not for

For

  • Companies running HubSpot that are weighing Salesforce, or wondering how long HubSpot will fit.

Not for

  • Teams on another CRM. This assessment covers HubSpot only.
  • Companies that have already bought Salesforce. Those start with a starter package.

Questions

Questions about choosing HubSpot or Salesforce

Will you just tell us to move to Salesforce?

No. The evaluation lays out both paths, and I am hired for the work on either one.

What do we need to prepare?

Nothing beyond the booking form: your HubSpot plan, how many people use it, what it is not doing for you, and your growth plan. Bring the person who owns the system and the person who owns the budget.

Do you need a login to our HubSpot?

No. The evaluation is built from the meeting and your answers on the booking form.

What happens after?

You decide. Make the changes yourselves, or hire Full Signal and the $799 comes off the price.

Make the decision once, with the facts in front of you.

If a move is coming, each month you wait adds data to move and workarounds to undo. If it is not, you can stop having the conversation.
