What you leave with

How it runs

Set it up, use it for a week, fix what broke.

  1. Session 1 · Dec 10

    Set up your job

    Each person sets up their own use case live, on their own work, with Rachael on hand to get it running.

  2. The week between

    Use it for real

    You run it on your actual week and note where it slips, so the second session works on what happened, not on guesses.

  3. Session 2 · Dec 17

    Fix what broke

    Bring what went wrong. We fix it together, and you leave with the job working.

After every session you get the recording and a step-by-step written guide, so missing part of a session doesn't leave you behind. Questions between sessions go in a shared Slack group.

Who it's for

Owners and operators who sell their own expertise.

If clients pay for what you know, and the same handful of jobs eats your week, this is built around you. You don't need to be technical. You need a paid Claude plan and one or two jobs you'd like off your plate.

Not for anyone looking for coding or automation-platform training, or for people who sell AI services.

Questions

Which Claude plan do I need?

A paid Claude plan, in place before the first session. If you're not sure which one fits, book the free Q&A call and ask.

What kind of job can I bring?

A job you do every week that follows the same steps: the client update, the proposal, the follow-up emails. You pick one or two from a short menu, so everyone gets hands-on time.

How much time does it take?

Two live 90-minute sessions, plus using your setup on your own work in the week between.

What if I miss part of a session?

You get the recording and the written guide after every session, and you can ask questions in the Slack group.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Everything is set up in plain English, with no code.

What happens after?

You keep everything you built. When you're ready for the next job, the Signal Tune-Up is the monthly way to keep building with Rachael.
