Iris AI · Your Salesforce admin

A Salesforce admin for your team, without the full-time hire.

Iris is an AI admin being designed to run on your own Salesforce. Ask her for a change and she will ask what you mean, check it against how your system was built and why, and keep a record of who asked and the reason. A monthly subscription.

Example

Add a "Technical Review" stage to opportunities.

You have two opportunity types, New Business and Renewal. Which one is this for?

New Business.

New Business runs five stages today. Technical Review was left out on purpose, to keep smaller deals moving. Do you still want it added?

Yes. The sales lead approved it for larger deals.

Noted. Requested by you, approved by the sales lead, for larger New Business deals. That goes in the history with today's date.

An example of how Iris is being designed to work, with mock data. Not a live system.

Without an admin

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • A manager asks for a new opportunity stage. Nobody remembers why the stages are set up the way they are.
  • A field gets changed on a busy afternoon, and a report breaks a week later.
  • The person who set it all up left, and the reasons left with them.
  • You need an admin for a few hours a month, and the role is hired by the year. A full-time Salesforce administrator averages about $91,600 a year in the United States.

Source for the salary line: Indeed, Salesforce administrator salary in United States, read 2026-11-20 (average base salary $91,621, updated November 15, 2026).

Being designed now

What Iris is being designed to do

  • Handle some configuration work.
  • Ask before she builds. Ask for a new opportunity stage and she will ask which opportunity type it belongs to, if you have more than one.
  • Flag when a request conflicts with something built earlier.
  • Tell you what is built today and the decisions behind it, then check you still want the change.
  • Keep the history of who asked for each change and why, so the knowledge stays with the company when people move on.

How it works

A monthly subscription, separate from the starter packages.

Tell us about your Salesforce and we will tell you what Iris would cover and what it costs.

Iris is being designed to run on Claude, so your team will need a Claude plan. New to Claude? We can set it up for you. Contact us.

You stay in charge

Built to check with you first

  • She is AI, and she will be labelled that way everywhere she appears.
  • She is designed to check with you before a change goes ahead.
  • Every change will carry a name and a reason.

For and not for

For

  • Teams running Salesforce with no full-time admin.

Not for

  • Companies with their own admin team.
  • Companies that have not set up Salesforce yet. Those start with a starter package.
